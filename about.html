ABOUT THE PROJECT

College Event Management

CampusEvents is a frontend-based College Event Management Website designed to make it easier for students to discover and participate in college events.

What We Provide


01

Discover Events

Students can explore upcoming technical, cultural, sports and other college events.

02

Event Details

View important information such as date, time, venue, activities and event rules.

03

Easy Registration

Students can fill out a simple registration form to participate in an event.
